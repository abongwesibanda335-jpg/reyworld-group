About the Group

Different businesses.
One wider vision.

REYWORLD GROUP is a growing business portfolio bringing different ventures together under one wider identity. The group spans several industries, with each business maintaining its own purpose, services and character.

Our portfolio currently includes construction and renovation, swimming pool services, automotive services, vehicle sales, music and fashion. Together, these businesses represent different areas of creativity, practical work and enterprise.

REYWORLD is built around the idea that businesses can develop in different directions while still contributing to one larger vision — building businesses, creating opportunities and developing something that can last.
