Who We Are

One group.
Multiple directions.

REYWORLD GROUP brings together a collection of businesses working across different industries. The current portfolio includes Tony & Brian Projects, Magma Pools, Colmad Auto, Auto Sales, MOB Records and Slatt Clothing.

These ventures cover practical services, automotive work, consumer services, music and fashion. While their industries differ, each business contributes to the wider REYWORLD identity through its own skills, audience and opportunities.

The group is not limited to the businesses that exist today. REYWORLD is intended to develop as opportunities arise, allowing new ventures, partnerships and business ideas to become part of the portfolio over time.

That means the portfolio can grow without losing the wider vision: building businesses, creating opportunities and developing a legacy that can extend beyond one industry.

The REYWORLD Difference

Built to develop.
Designed to grow.

REYWORLD GROUP brings different types of business under one identity without forcing every venture to look or operate the same way.

A construction company has different customers and priorities from a pool company. An automotive business has a different purpose from a record label or clothing brand. REYWORLD allows each venture to retain that individuality.

At the same time, being part of a wider group creates a shared platform for development, visibility and future opportunities. The portfolio can therefore expand into new areas while maintaining a consistent wider vision.

Today there are six businesses. Tomorrow, there may be more. The growth of the portfolio is part of the story.
